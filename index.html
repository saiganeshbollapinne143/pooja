Our Story

Three generations of
the sacred art of scent

What began as a single hand-rolling table in Mysuru is today one of the region's most loved agarbatti houses. We still do things the slow way — masala blends mixed by family recipes, sticks rolled by hand, and shade-dried over 72 hours so the fragrance matures deeply.

  • Pure masala base — no charcoal, no synthetic fillers, only herbs, resins and flower powders.
  • Essential-oil perfuming — fragrances matured for 21 days before packing.
  • Fair livelihoods — employing 60+ women artisans from nearby villages.

From forest to shrine

The making of a Sugandh Sagar stick

01

Blending

Sandalwood powder, herbs, resins and flower dust are mixed with natural gum into a fragrant masala.

02

Hand-rolling

Artisans roll each stick by hand around bamboo cores — the craft our family has honed for 27 years.

03

Shade-drying

Sticks rest 72 hours in shaded racks, then absorb essential oils for a full 21 days.

04

Quality check

Every batch is test-burned for aroma depth, burn time and ash quality before packing.
